🚣 QUICK START - iPhone Setup
=============================

STEP 1: Generate Icons
-----------------------
1. Open "ICON_GENERATOR.html" in your browser
2. Click "Generate All Icons" button
3. 8 icon files will download to your Downloads folder
4. Move all icon files to the "icons" folder in this directory

STEP 2: Follow Detailed Instructions
-------------------------------------
Open "GITHUB_SETUP_INSTRUCTIONS.md" for complete step-by-step guide.

The instructions will walk you through:
- Creating a GitHub account/repository
- Uploading your files
- Enabling GitHub Pages
- Installing on your iPhone

Total time: ~30-45 minutes for first-time setup

Your app will be accessible at:
https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/rowing-tracker/

Good luck! 🚣‍♂️
